# User-confirmed hall-ticket schedule verification authority

On 2026-11-18, during Phase 3 continuation, the assistant asked who may authenticate
an individual faculty hall-ticket scheduled exam date for quota transfer, referencing
master §9.2's requirement for confirmed authority. The user's answer was **“HoD”**.
This is the recorded project-policy clarification; it is not a claim of a separate
signed institutional document or a new certificate/compliance authority.

The current formally appointed department HoD (including the existing acting-HoD
appointment semantics) may independently authenticate the official hall ticket for
the exact eligible applicant and offering. Self-verification is prohibited. Preserve
source reference/identifier, document digest, observation time, verified date,
verifier and office, version, rationale and audit. A faculty assertion is unverified.
The finding is MANUAL_HOD_AUTHENTICATED; it is not a provider API verification.

Apply confirmed V2.3 to that person's counted live allocation only: same charging
topic, target published annual capacity, atomic balanced movement; retain and flag
when capacity is unavailable. The individual verified date takes precedence for
that person's schedule over a provider-wide forecast. Further individual corrections
require a new effective verification revision. Never alter another person's date,
original submission, historical movement, actual exam attendance or certificate.

This clarification does not resolve IO-02, P-54, Q51, emerging-tech exception authority,
retroactive cross-topic movement, indefinite failed-transfer remedies or other
unratified evidence-verification responsibilities.
