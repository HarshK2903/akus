# AKUS NPTEL — Principal Product/UI Design Specification

Edition: UI/UX Handoff 1.0, 15 November 2026. Design scope: responsive LIGHT-THEME institutional NPTEL module of AKUS. This is a specification, not application code. The implementation handoff bundles the latest verified AKUS master implementation baseline V2.4 in full, so its recipient does not need this conversation.

## DELIVERABLE 1 — Project-to-UI requirements and authority reconciliation

**Project:** AKUS (Automated Knowledge Upgrade System), institutional faculty NPTEL participation, quota governance, approvals, evidence, annual compliance, subject-wise Silver compliance, separation and reproducible audits. Not a student ERP, a general-purpose HR suite, or an NPTEL provider website. Scale design target: college-sized multi-department institution; exact headcount is not a ratified deployment constraint.

**Precedence:** Latest explicit institutional amendments override incompatible older numbered Q-01–Q-50 decisions. Preserve old Q policies as historical versions but do not implement superseded role or quota paths as live functionality. In this UI, a role or action is enabled only if the server proves current authority and the applicable policy is confirmed.

### Currently active institutional role model

- **Faculty (F):** eligible NPTEL participant; draft/submit, view own applications, provide evidence, see annual/Silver compliance, request legitimate withdrawal, maintain historical view. Ordinary application review: DRC Head -> HoD.
- **DRC Head (D):** an eligible FACULTY member with all faculty responsibilities, plus independent academic review of OTHER faculty's applications in authorized department. The DRC Head's OWN application bypasses DRC self-review and goes DIRECTLY to HoD for final decision.
- **HoD / formally appointed acting HoD (H):** exempt from compulsory NPTEL participation in the office; not a personal NPTEL applicant in the current design. Authorized departmental final review, course-to-primary-topic mapping, annual topic quota publication/revision, failed-attempt outcome verification, eligible separation closure, ambiguity review and departmental reporting. Must never be simultaneously the DRC Head; validate effective-dated cross-department office non-overlap.
- **Principal (P):** top hierarchy with institutional-wide READ/oversight on quotas, decisions, compliance, audits, reports and unresolved cases. Exempt from compulsory NPTEL and no personal applicant workflow. Global oversight is not a general mutation or self-approval privilege. The old emerging-technology Principal approval route is an OPEN POLICY CONFLICT; do not turn it into a live button until ratified.
- **Admin (A):** authoritative, audited institutional staff/org/academic-year/teaching-assignment configuration and provider adapter operations. No academic approval or departmental quota write from Admin role alone; a person with other roles must separately satisfy those role grants. Faculty MOOC Coordinator is a scoped faculty designation, not a sixth universal RBAC role.

### Latest binding topic-quota rules

1. Exactly ONE department-specific, HoD-approved PRIMARY charging topic for each (department, canonical NPTEL course) across its offerings while that mapping version is effective. Original provider MULTI-CATEGORY labels remain visible; applicants cannot choose a convenient category. A different department may approve a different topic. Revisions are effective-dated, audited and evidence-backed. **Retroactive movement of active seats following mapping revision remains UNDECIDED**; show impact/read-only historical reservations without automatically migrating.
2. One published shared pool per (department, canonical charging topic, July-1-to-June-30 academic year); numerous courses/offerings may share it. Cap owned and editable by authorized HoD, but not reduced below currently counted occupancy. Unmapped course/topic or unpublished annual pool => draft and deduplicated mapping/quota intake, never a successful application submission or HELD seat.
3. At valid submit, provider's CURRENT officially PUBLISHED/SCHEDULED exam date determines INITIAL pool year; enrollment cutoff, exam-registration cutoff, duration and other eligibility are validated. Atomically reserve 1 counted seat with application creation/submission/audit. The published exam date is provisional; it does not prove exam attendance.
4. Verified provider reschedule crossing July–June boundary => atomically migrate a currently counted reservation to target published pool for same department/topic, only if capacity exists, preserving original/current pool history. If destination missing/unpublished/full, keep original seat counted and show a transparent transfer reconciliation case; no double count. Completion academic-year credit later follows ACTUAL verified exam date Q-35 regardless of whether transfer succeeded.
5. Distinguish category versus canonical course versus individual offering versus subject relevance; those are different records and decision contexts. Distinguish DRC/HOD academic REVIEW from HoD TOPIC MAPPING approval from quota publication from evidence verification.

### Invariant-first UX

Every submission, approval, rejection, quota revision, mapping revision, evidence verification, separation closure, historical reassessment and transfer must be represented as a server-authorized action with explicit after-state, immutable timeline and real error recovery. Never pretend that AKUS approval enrolls a person on the external NPTEL platform or validates an uploaded certificate automatically. Never expose 'switch topic' choice to faculty. Never hide an unresolved policy behind an automatic decision.

## DELIVERABLE 2 — Visual design research and selected direction

**Chosen aesthetic:** 'Institutional Operations' — restrained light enterprise SaaS with high-density but readable information, deliberate borders, one blue emphasis color, few status pills, sparse functional icons and well-aligned data tables. Not a financial trading dashboard, not university marketing, not a generic AI template. UI inspiration is LIMITED to public design principles, never brand copying.

- GitHub Primer: PageHeader, nested navigation/list detail, precise action hierarchy, contextual content separation: https://primer.style/product/components/ and https://primer.style/product/ui-patterns/navigation/ .
- Atlassian navigation system: globally consistent top navigation and app-specific collapsible side navigation: https://atlassian.design/components/navigation-system/migration-guide .
- IBM Carbon: searchable/filterable sortable operational data tables, with row detail pages when work becomes complex: https://v10.carbondesignsystem.com/components/data-table/usage/ .
- U.S. Web Design System: explicit step indicators for genuine linear multi-page forms: https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/step-indicator/ .
- WCAG 2.2: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/ ; meet AA, prioritize keyboard focus, understandable errors, target sizing and text contrast.

Only inspect these as documented design patterns; do not claim exact product visuals were copied or rights to proprietary assets. AKUS must have its own design tokens, type scale and identity.

### Product identity and visual voice

Working UI name: 'AKUS | NPTEL'. Top left a compact typographic AKUS mark and institutional label if provided by deployment configuration; do not fabricate an official crest. Tone: formal, plain English, explain blockers concretely. Use full labels 'Awaiting HoD review', 'Exam date unverified', 'Topic mapping pending', 'Quota transfer blocked' instead of ambiguous colors or cryptic acronyms.

### Visual anti-pattern exclusions

No giant gradient hero, emoji menu, stock photographs on dashboards, glassmorphism, purple/cyan neon, excessive rounded cards, huge KPI tiles, charts without decision value, dense modals for core tasks, skeletons that lie about loaded state, randomly chosen spacing, every page as repeated card grid, placeholder metrics or buttons without server action.

## DELIVERABLE 3 — Sitemap and role navigation

**Global shell:** Top bar (product, institution, breadcrumbs-compatible context, search, notifications, help, profile); left role-filtered side nav; content area; optional narrow contextual inspector only on pages where records benefit from linked evidence/history.

**Shared:** Home; NPTEL Catalog; My Applications; My Compliance; Tasks (scoped); Notifications; Profile; Help/Policy Glossary. HoD/Principal exemption removes 'My NPTEL' applicant/compliance actions; Admin role alone does not add personal NPTEL duties.

**Faculty:** Overview; Catalog (Courses -> Course -> Offering); My applications (List -> Draft workflow -> Submission receipt -> Detail / Evidence / Quota / History); Prior Attempts; Participation Evidence; Annual Compliance (Year detail); Subject-wise Silver (List -> Obligation detail); Notifications.

**DRC Head:** All Faculty navigation plus 'DRC Reviews' list -> Review detail -> Historic DRC decisions; own applications remain in My Applications and route directly HoD.

**HoD:** Department Overview; Application Reviews -> Decision detail; Topic Governance -> Mapping Queue / Mapping Detail / Mapping Review / Taxonomy; Annual Topic Quotas -> Pool List / Pool Detail / Publish or Revise / Ledger; Exception Work -> Blocked Quota Transfers / Transfer Detail / Outcome Verification / Integrity Reviews / Separation Reviews / Chronology Review; Reports -> Department Compliance / Quota / Applications; Decision History.

**Principal:** Institutional Overview; Departments -> Department Detail; Institution-wide Applications; Topics & Quotas; Compliance -> Annual / Silver; Exception Monitoring; Audit & Historical Reports. All default read-only; pending special-emerging-tech approval authority is not enabled until policy resolved.

**Admin:** Institutional Data -> Staff / Staff Detail / Employment Episodes / Role Appointments / Teaching Assignments / Departments / Subjects / Academic Years; NPTEL Reference Data -> Provider Sync / Offering Provenance / Canonical Topics / Course Identity; Operations -> Jobs / Notifications / Integration Health / Audit (restricted). No HoD topic mapping/quotas academic approval through Admin-only rights.

**Utilities:** `/login`, `/auth/callback`, `/access-denied`, `/system-unavailable`, `/not-found`. Notification links deep-link directly to authorized target and preserve filter/back context. Invalid/expired office appointments must remove actions and redirect/revalidate, not rely on cached navigation visibility.

## DELIVERABLE 7 — Complete role-based journeys and transitions

**J1 Faculty ordinary first application:** Sign in -> Faculty overview -> Catalog search -> Course detail -> Exact offering (source dates/provider multi-category displayed) -> New wizard choose -> Eligibility -> Evidence -> Review & submit (read-only HoD-approved topic, scheduled exam AY, published shared pool) -> server atomic reserve receipt -> My applications detail with DRC pending -> DRC review -> HoD final review -> distinct enrollment evidence page -> exam/cert evidence -> annual/Silver assessment. On a missing mapping or quota, do NOT display success: save draft and open HoD mapping/quota intake. On no-seat race, show refreshed published occupancy and safe 'Return to draft'.

**J2 DRC Head own application:** DRC signs in -> Faculty overview (all faculty duties) -> Draft/submit -> final review route states 'HoD final review — DRC Head applicant, DRC self-review bypassed' -> HoD decision -> own application timeline. DRC cannot open their own case in DRC review queue, even via direct URL. A DRC review of another faculty remains available only when office effective.

**J3 Independent DRC review:** DRC queue -> review detail -> inspect relevance, provider dates, evidence, prior outcome, application submitted before actual enrollment -> enter required decision rationale -> Approve or Reject -> server revalidates appointment and policy -> confirmation -> updated queue and audited timeline. **J4 HoD final approval:** HoD queue -> task detail -> DRC decision where appropriate or direct DRC applicant path -> inspect published shared topic pool and actual external evidence -> approve/reject -> server transitions and audit -> task disappears from queue; former HoD cannot act. No HoD applicant workflow.

**J5 Multi-category topic mapping setup:** Faculty offering detail sees provider multiple category labels and missing mapping -> Save draft / Request mapping -> deduplicated HoD inbox -> mapping request detail -> HoD selects one academically justified canonical topic, evidence and rationale -> approval -> faculty draft now has read-only primary topic -> if annual pool not published, HoD quota creation -> faculty can then submit. **J6 Topic quota publication:** HoD annual pool listing -> choose FY July–June and approved topic -> draft cap -> publish with rationale -> concurrency-safe occupancy validation -> pool detail/ledger. Increasing published cap permitted; decreasing below occupancy blocked. No direct seat editing.

**J7 Cross-year schedule change:** Authorized provider adapter verifies new published exam date -> case moved from 2026–27 into 2027–28 target year -> transaction checks published target same department/topic pool, locks both and atomically migrates counted seat when available -> application detail shows original vs current and movement history. **J8 Mapping revision with active reservations:** HoD mapping detail -> Revise mapping -> explain affected old reservations and preserve versioned history -> new submissions use approved revised topic as of effective policy -> old counted allocations and impact case remain visible; NEVER auto-migrate old pools because this is still unratified. **J9 Outcome verification and repeat attempt:** Faculty previous attempts -> course outcome unresolved -> upload documentary result -> HoD verification case -> documented no-certificate result or valid cert or unresolved. No seat until later fresh application passes rules. A corrected old result after later approval opens Q33 integrity case and pauses only newer compliance credit; quota preserved during review; Q23 release if invalidated; Q34 normal evaluation if upheld.

**J10 Integrity chronology conflict:** Current approved app -> evidence reveals actual enrollment earlier than AKUS submission -> formal Q22 integrity case -> suspended application compliance recognition, counted quota retained -> independent authorized decision uphold/invalidate -> invalidate Q23 atomic release with historical audit; do not silently remove external certificate.

**J11 Annual compliance:** Faculty or authorized reviewer -> annual year selector -> server groups achievements by ACTUAL verified exam date -> one qualifying >=8wk or 2 distinct 4wk same-year courses, valid certificates below Silver okay -> confirmation only when verified -> as-of versus latest historical view if late certificate. Exclude HoD/Principal from mandatory denominator.

**J12 Silver lifecycle and rehire:** Admin records effective-dated teaching assignment -> obligation per faculty+canonical subject+episode, deadline two calendar years later -> faculty Silver table shows original start/deadline; ending teaching does not reset -> permanent separation closes active monitoring preserving status snapshot -> rehire new episode and new assignment creates new obligation -> late valid relevant Silver result from pre-separation exam can establish retrospective historical fulfillment without reopening monitoring. **J13 Three separation paths:** Admin authorized effective separation record -> application reconciliation -> Q43 pre-enrollment pre-commencement auto close/release, Q44 enrolled pre-commencement review with seat counted until HoD final close, Q45 after commencement auto close/release; Q46 uses actual event time not processing; Q47 same date-only ambiguity -> HoD chronology review, counted seat pending, documented Q47 close/release if still unprovable. ## DELIVERABLE 9 — Production design review and correction log

1. **Avoid template dashboard syndrome:** Dashboard uses a compact priority strip and real work lists rather than cloned giant cards and stock charts. Topic/quota and audit pages use full-width tables; forms use narrow reading widths.
2. **Avoid false authority:** HoD and Principal are exempt; no personal NPTEL form. DRC personal case bypasses own review. DRC and HoD never overlap. Principal global read-only; special emerging-technology approval route remains policy pending.
3. **Avoid domain conflation:** Topic mapping is not course tagging, quota pool is not per-offering quota, provider metadata is not enrollment proof, verification is not automatically credit, exam date at submission is not actual exam date.
4. **Avoid quota lies:** Faculty submission UI shows current published pool and seat availability as of read, but repeats server validation on commit; after a transfer block UI explicitly shows original seat retained and target NOT reserved.
5. **Avoid destructive history:** Every record has detail/history and effective time versus recorded time; no silent changes to closed applications, old mapping versions, old report snapshots or separated faculty status.
6. **Avoid navigational shortcuts:** Complex applications, outcome review, mapping review and evidence management have dedicated routes. Small confirmation actions use dialogs; core decisions are not performed in generic dropdown modals.
7. **Avoid role mixing:** Global role indicator is information, not an authorization switch; deep links validate on backend. Separate Admin source management from HoD academic judgments.
8. **Avoid premature policy choices:** Open Q51 one Silver certificate across multiple subjects, map-revision retroactivity, indefinite failed quota transfer remediation, faculty promotion-to-HoD active-app behavior, emerging technology Principal route and other unresolved items must be visible as pending where relevant, never silently resolved.
9. **Avoid decorative analytics:** Only charts showing meaningful time trends; provide accessible tables and clear source dates. No percent metrics lacking denominator definition.
10. **Security/accessibility:** WCAG AA contrast and keyboard navigation, no hidden-only validation, no unauthorized record existence leak, reviewed error/empty/skeleton/mobile states.

This revision governance is distinct from confirmed quota cross-YEAR transfer.
- nptel_offerings: immutable exact provider offering ID (e.g. noc26_cs98), canonical course FK, duration_weeks, published start_date/end_date/enrollment_deadline/exam_registration_deadline/published_or_scheduled_exam_date, timezone and date precision, authoritative source state and freshness. - canonical_course_alias_reviews: evidence and adjudicated mappings; unresolved equivalence blocks no-repeat-sensitive submissions.

TOPIC QUOTA / ALLOCATION:
- academic_years: authoritative configured July 1–June 30 year IDs; validate no overlaps. Initial quota-charge resolver uses the exact offering's SOURCE-VERIFIED scheduled_exam_date_at_submission to select the INITIAL pool, saving immutable source/version/date and original quota-pool ID; not application submitted_at or course start. A later verified cross-year schedule change invokes V2.3 capacity-checked atomic move; source/target occupancy and movement ledger auditable, failed move retains current pool. Q35 actual verified exam date for final annual compliance is a separate fact. No automatic quota creation or publication.
- department_topic_quotas: UNIQUE (department_id, canonical_topic_id, academic_year_id), nonnegative maximum slots, DRAFT/PUBLISHED/RETIRED, current HoD authority, revision, published_at. One shared pool covers multiple courses and offerings mapped to that topic within its legally charged July–June year. The quota itself is never auto-created with arbitrary default capacity at rollover.
- topic_intake_requests: proposed topic, observed provider tags, faculty draft IDs, request evidence, dedupe/reconciliation, HoD setup task, created_at, state and outcomes. Topic intake request is NOT a submitted NPTEL application, and reserves NO seat.
- topic_quota_revisions: append-only before/after limit, HoD actor, decision-time authority, reason, revision time and underlying evidence.
- application_allocations: exactly one currently counted allocation per eligible active application; stable application and exact offering/canonical course/topic/department identity; IMMUTABLE original submitted pool ID/year and MUTABLE authoritative currently charged pool pointer plus state HELD/CONFIRMED/RETAINED/RELEASED and monotonic version. On authorized V2.3 transfer preserve full source/target allocation segment history and atomic migration transaction. Never lose the originally charged pool or represent the same seat simultaneously in two counted pools.
- quota_ledger: append-only occupancy transitions including V2.3 matched TRANSFER_OUT(source) and TRANSFER_IN(target), both linked by unique migration transaction/event and the exact provider evidence/version; source application/event, actor/service, policy, transaction/correlation ID, old/new counts and decision time. Every migration is an auditable double-sided movement, never a naked year-ID overwrite.
- quota_transfer_cases: application/allocation, immutable original submission pool, current source pool, intended target pool/year, old/new scheduled exam dates and source versions, status [PENDING_SOURCE_VERIFICATION, TRANSFER_RECONCILIATION_PENDING, TRANSFERRED, SUPERSEDED, CLOSED], blocking reason [MISSING_POOL, UNPUBLISHED_POOL, FULL_POOL, STALE_EVENT, INELIGIBLE_STATE, CONFLICT], created/updated times, last attempt and current reviewer scope. Unique active transfer case per application+verified schedule revision; retries idempotent; source-provider timestamps are not institutional approval times.
- quota_transfer_events: unique event ID/provider revision, application, old pool, new pool, evidence refs, migration transaction ID, timestamp and immutable outcome; support repeated transfers and as-of pool occupancy reporting.
- Lock the shared topic-pool quota row on submission, release and limit changes, with validated occupied_count or locked ledger/allocations. For V2.3 migration, lock application/allocation and BOTH pool rows in stable globally deterministic order; validate versions and target PUBLISHED capacity AFTER locks, then atomically debit source and credit target with paired ledger event and one current allocation pointer update. Enforce nonnegative source occupancy, target no overbooking and no double count. Retained/unresolved previous-year allocations stay counted in their CURRENT pool unless a distinct authorized transition applies. Mere year rollover does not move them.
- No faculty-directed quota choice, topic final approval or limit edit. HoD publishes a numeric department+topic+target-year pool. Missing/unreliable scheduled date or unpublished pool at INITIAL admission => DRAFT/EVIDENCE_PENDING, NO submitted application or seat. Differences between submission/start year and exam year are acceptable when scheduled exam year known. AFTER successful submission, a provider-verified cross-year RESCHEDULE invokes ratified V2.3: transfer if target PUBLISHED and free, otherwise keep the source counted and flag reconciliation; NEVER retroactively reinterpret the initial submission as not submitted merely because target year is full.

ORDINARY ELIGIBLE FACULTY APPLICANT:
  Faculty submits -> current authorized DRC Head independent academic check -> current authorized HoD final academic/institutional approval.

DRC HEAD AS APPLICANT:

HOD AND PRINCIPAL:
  Neither participates as personal NPTEL applicant. No own-course intake, personal quota reservation, annual compulsory NPTEL obligation or subject-wise compulsory Silver workflow while exempt. HoD remains academic reviewer, quota owner, designated verifier and institutional operations authority. Principal sees institution-wide permitted data, oversight and audit; this DOES NOT mean Principal can approve everyone or edit quota/certificates. The old Q10/Q12–Q15/Q31/Q32 workflow cases are SUPERSEDED AND MUST NOT BE EXECUTED.

STRICT ROLE APPOINTMENT INVARIANT:
  Faculty may become DRC Head. DRC Head and HoD/acting HoD may NEVER be the same person at overlapping effective times, even in different departments. Validate at office appointment/import and revalidate at submission/reviewer decision. Office transitions may have historic successive appointments but never concurrent overlap.

EMERGING TECHNOLOGY EXCEPTION:
  The earlier default said Principal gives special emerging-technology final permission; the newer clarification frames Principal as top hierarchy with global visibility and declares DRC Head's own courses approved ONLY by HoD. Do not invent whether a Principal decision still exists for ordinary faculty emerging-tech requests. Record this as OPEN policy. Let normal Faculty/DRC applications proceed only through an explicitly approved route; for a required unapproved exception, retain safe POLICY_PENDING, with no unsupported auto final authorization. Do not ask Principal to approve DRC Head NPTEL applications by default.

AUTHORIZATION REQUIREMENTS:
- Google OIDC subject maps to authorized institutional person; effective-dated offices and employment provide real permissions. Only eligible Faculty/DRC Head may originate new NPTEL applications.
- At the moment of each action, verify current employment, applicant eligibility, applicant-versus-reviewer identity, department scope, exact task stage, current HoD/DRC office validity, application state/version and policy preconditions.
- Route topology is determined from applicant's verified designation at successful submission and versioned policy. Task execution re-resolves the current authorized officeholder; past decisions are never rewritten.
- Missing DRC Head for an ordinary Faculty application or missing HoD for either applicant class results in a pending task, occupied seat retained and appropriate alerts; do not automatically grant Admin/Principal permission.
- If a Faculty is promoted to HoD while a personal application is active, do not silently approve, withdraw or auto exempt the older application; create a controlled POLICY_PENDING case until the institution defines treatment, while safeguarding no self-review and historical facts.
- The Principal global overview may show all departments' applications, quotas, exception queues and reporting based on authenticated audit/record privacy scope; preserve access logs and limit exports of sensitive evidence as appropriate. Oversight has no automatic academic edit power.
- The authorized HoD may publish/edit topic quotas and verify previous unsuccessful results for eligible Faculty/DRC Head, including DRC Head applicants (Q30). HoD cannot self-create a personal application.
- Admin manages appointments and reference data via authorized source workflows but cannot approve NPTEL applications or fabricate provider facts.
- Integrity cases Q22/Q33 require a conflict-free independently authorized reviewer; unresolved exceptional authority results in AUTHORITY_POLICY_PENDING, not invented Principal override.
- Deny self-review, stale appointments, concurrent DRC+HoD identity, invalid Applicant role, role escalation, and unauthorized across-department edits on SERVER; log denied security-sensitive actions.

8. QUOTA AND APPLICATION STATE-MACHINE ENGINEERING
======================================================================

MODEL THREE RELATED STATE MACHINES (not one overloaded status string):
A. Application: DRAFT -> SUBMITTED/UNDER_REVIEW -> APPROVED/REJECTED/WITHDRAWN/SEPARATION_REVIEW/CLOSED_DUE_TO_SEPARATION/INVALIDATED etc. Exact states can vary if transitions preserve policy.
B. Allocation: NONE -> HELD -> CONFIRMED -> RETAINED or RELEASED; counting status and history separate from application status.
C. External participation: UNKNOWN/NOT_ENROLLED/ENROLLED/EXAM_PENDING/EXAM_WRITTEN/OUTCOME_UNKNOWN/NO_CERT/CERT_VALID/RESULT_CORRECTED. This is an evidence-driven set of independent facts; avoid impossible single linear simplification.

CRITICAL SUBMIT TRANSACTION:
1. Validate signed-in, currently eligible institutional person and employment episode; correct department and course; provider offering; draft data; declarations; score-sharing 'yes'; policy eligibility.
2. Verify actual/suspected enrollment chronology: submission must precede external enrollment (Q21); self-declaration plus evidence reconciliation, not proof by checkbox alone.
3. Verify no valid previous certificate for canonical course; prior outcomes conclusively verified if needed; no other active canonical-course application (Q27–29).
4. Resolve exact NPTEL offering and canonical course, source-verified duration, course start/end, enrollment deadline, exam-registration deadline and PUBLISHED/SCHEDULED EXAM DATE as of submit. Determine the official JULY–JUNE year containing scheduled exam. Read the ONE currently EFFECTIVE HoD-approved DEPARTMENT+CANONICAL-COURSE primary charging-topic mapping and its revision, then the department+APPROVED_TOPIC+TARGET_YEAR published quota. Faculty-selected topic or provider tag is NEVER authoritative. Missing/unapproved department-course mapping => create deduplicated HoD mapping-intake task and remain DRAFT/PENDING_MAPPING; existing mapping but topic missing/alias unresolved => controlled topic intake; mapping approved but annual quota unpublished => DRAFT/PENDING_QUOTA. None creates a Q02 submission or HELD seat. Revalidate mapping version/authority and source date together at transaction commit. If published scheduled exam date is unavailable/unverifiable or target year unconfigured, preserve draft/evidence pending. Do not block solely because submit/start occur in different years. Preview planned date does not prove actual exam attendance.
5. Require a PUBLISHED HoD-owned department+topic+ACADEMIC-YEAR quota, then lock that shared pool (including all other courses/offerings assigned to that topic and year), check remaining capacity, and enforce canonical-course active uniqueness using stable lock order.
6. Insert immutable submitted snapshot (including charged topic, official charge academic year and exact quota-pool ID), DRC->HoD or direct HoD route plan, HELD topic-pool allocation and quota ledger event, audit and outbox notifications in ONE DB transaction.
7. Commit; only after commit notify frontend success and start any external side effects.
8. Ensure client retries using Idempotency-Key cannot produce duplicate application or double hold. Respond with deterministic conflict and capacity info where safe.

REVIEW / REJECTION:
- DRC/HoD rejection -> transition terminal and RELEASE allocation Q06; preserve original reasons, actor/authority, decision time and audit.
- HoD final approval on eligible ordinary Faculty or DRC Head application -> transition to final institutionally approved when valid route completes; HELD -> CONFIRMED as appropriate. Principal has oversight, not routine final approval.
- If approval is late relative to provider deadline, check Q19 and Q20; legitimate verified existing NPTEL enrollment after AKUS submission can establish valid external path. Do NOT invent NPTEL enrollment when external deadline closes.

VOLUNTARY WITHDRAWAL:
- Before external enrollment AND before commencement: release under Q03 if voluntary withdrawal is valid.
- After external enrollment BEFORE commencement: HoD verifies and authorizes if releasing Q04.
- AFTER commencement: counted RETAINED even if never externally enrolled Q05; application closes for activity but occupancy stays counted. A later rejection or integrity invalidation, where legitimate under policy, has distinct release effects.
- Fresh application after REJECTED or pre-enrollment/pre-commencement WITHDRAWN is a new independent application, never resurrect original Q24/25. Enforce remaining quota, time and canonical uniqueness.

PERMANENT FACULTY SEPARATION:
- Q43, Q44, Q45 and Q47 govern based on authoritative actual event chronology; Q46 ensures processing time never reclassifies. See detailed scenario tests.
- Automatic closure for Q43/Q45 does NOT imply an NPTEL provider withdrawal or a fraud finding.
- When Q44 HoD review is pending, counted reservation persists even after course commences; ordinary approvals blocked.
- On Q47 ambiguity, hold quota and route to current HoD; if order remains unprovable, record distinct INDETERMINATE finding and authorized release, not fake chronology.
- Genuine resignation announcement without effective date, temporary leave, intra-institutional transfer and date-only contradictory evidence cannot trigger automatic permanent-separation closure.

UNIQUE CONCURRENCY GUARANTEES:
- Two different faculty competing for last seat: exactly one succeeds.
- One faculty concurrently submits two offerings of same canonical course: at most one active successful submission.
- Concurrent HoD topic-pool quota decrease and last-seat application (possibly DIFFERENT courses in SAME topic): capacity never violated.
- Concurrent approval and separation closure: one valid linearized final state.
- Concurrent rejection/withdrawal/invalidation/closure: release exactly once, terminal reason preserved.
- Concurrent evidence correction and compliance assessment: no unsupported affirmative credit based on stale facts.
- Active application uniqueness may use partial unique index over canonical person ID + canonical course ID for state in active family; ensure any split table/derived active-state solution provably enforces race safety.
- Never use 'check count then insert' outside a protective lock/unique constraint as quota enforcement.

9.3 NO-REPEAT / ATTEMPT OUTCOME RESOLUTION:
- Scope eligibility to PERMANENT PERSON ID + CANONICAL COURSE (not offering, not email, not employment episode).
- Valid external NPTEL certificate earned => future repeat prohibited Q28 even if <75%, even if the old course has expired or employee was rehired.
- Failure/no-certificate needs conclusive documented authorized verification. A faculty statement, expired course, no PDF, unknown result or merely not registered for exam must not be guessed as certified failure.
- No verified result => allow drafts, BLOCK submissions and seats Q29.
- Only eligible ordinary Faculty/DRC Head are applicants, and their previous unsuccessful attempt evidence goes to the current authorized distinct HoD under Q30. Older HoD/Principal applicant verifications Q31/Q32 are retired. Self-review denied.
- If provider retroactively changes failed to valid: retain original verification, compare corrected source and time, open Q33 formal review for later approved repeat, SUSPEND later application's institutional compliance recognition, RETAIN counted seat during review; final uphold allows narrowed Q34 eligibility, invalidation releases under Q23. Do not equate retroactive correction with dishonesty.

10. ANNUAL NPTEL COMPLIANCE ENGINE: EXACT SEMANTICS
======================================================================

10.1 ACADEMIC YEAR:
- Annual academic period = July 1 through June 30 (inclusive as dates). Example AY 2026–27 = 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30.
- Annual attribution date = actual verified examination date, NOT provider certificate issue/AKUS verification date Q35.
- A faculty may have course activity spanning academic years, but actual exam date controls annual attribution.
- Do not treat current calendar time, 'latest active session', or year rollover as an instruction to rewrite old attendance/compliance relationships.

10.2 TWO COMPLETION PATHWAYS:
Path A: ONE qualifying NPTEL course lasting >=8 weeks + exam written + genuine valid verified certificate + all applicable institutional/participation/score-sharing requirements. Silver >=75% is NOT mandatory for annual certification Q38.
Path B: TWO DISTINCT qualifying NPTEL 4-week courses + both exams physically written in SAME July–June year + genuine valid verified certificates for both + other applicable requirements. Silver >=75% NOT mandatory for either certificate Q36/Q37. No 4-week cross-year carry-forward.
- Do not secretly permit 6-week+2-week, 8-week combined from arbitrary segments, 1x4-week only, or cross-year pairing without confirmed exception.
- A completed 8-week course and a completed 4-week course might both exist; annual obligation is single effective fulfilled result, with traceable evidence choices, not two completions.
- The same certificate/achievement must not be duplicated as two separate 4-week entries. A single annual obligation cannot be counted twice.
- Distinct annual and Silver obligations may be assessed against the same valid achievement IF independently eligible.

10.3 ANNUAL STATE AND REPORTING:
- Annual statuses should distinguish NO_APPLICABLE_OBLIGATION / NOT_STARTED / IN_PROGRESS / EXAM_WRITTEN_PENDING_CERTIFICATE / VERIFIED_FULFILLED / NOT_FULFILLED / UNDER_INTEGRITY_REVIEW / POLICY_PENDING / HISTORICALLY_REASSESSED. Choose precise schema naming, preserve reason codes.
- A late certificate from an exam in June is credited to prior AY once verified in July or later; prior report-as-of pending state remains historically accurate.
- Existing verified enrollment pathway Q20 matters when institutional approval was given after provider enrollment; do not disqualify solely because approval came later if submission predated enrollment.
- Integrity review pending Q22/Q33 suspends institutional recognition. Provider certificate remains independent; do not erase it.
- Q48: after permanent separation, verification of authentic valid certificate for eligible PRE-separation exam may confirm historical annual obligation. Keep closed application and released seat closed/released; no employment or role reactivation.
- Whether annual report already formally signed-off must be reissued after later evidence is an undecided governance policy: preserve old snapshot, show latest revised view, require administrative publication/reissue workflow configured only after formal policy.

11. SUBJECT-WISE SILVER COMPLIANCE ENGINE: EXACT SEMANTICS
======================================================================

11.1 OBLIGATION CREATION:
- Each official first teaching assignment of a canonical subject in a given employment episode creates a distinct subject-wise Silver obligation, with original start = effective first assignment date and deadline = exactly two CALENDAR years later Q39/Q42.
- Must derive first date from official academic assignment history. Entry/import date is not effective teaching date.
- A continuing faculty who stops teaching a subject or is transferred but remains employed keeps same obligation and original deadline Q40.
- Returning to same subject in SAME employment episode does not create a second obligation. Genuine permanent separation and REHIRE creates a NEW episode and a new obligation only on new official subject assignment Q41/Q42.
- Don't ignore pre-existing authentic Silver achievements; evaluate them against new legitimate obligations instead of forcing unneeded recertification. Whether certificate reuse across MULTIPLE DISTINCT subjects is still Q51 unresolved.

11.2 SILVER EVALUATION:
- Institutional Silver threshold >=75% (confirmed user-defined AKUS requirement; do NOT assert NPTEL's own public Silver label always equals 75%). Store actual numerical score/credential classification and rule source.
- Achievement must be genuine, verified, academically RELEVANT to the specific canonical teaching subject, within the applicable deadline semantics, and from institutionally eligible participation.
- On-time determination = ACTUAL qualifying examination DATE <= original two-year subject deadline Q50; certificate issuance/verification may be later, with on-time fulfillment confirmed only after verification.
- Preserve EXAM_TIMELY_RESULT_PENDING state for evidence gaps. No automatic failed assumption from silence, and do not manufacture confirmation.
- A subject-course relevance mapping is evidence, not automatically credential legitimacy. Explicit relevance rationale should identify topic coverage/subject mapping; if reviewer authorization undecided, hold for approval rather than silently credit.
- Separate 'deadline met', 'certificate valid', 'subject relevance', 'institutionally approved/eligible', 'review suspension' and 'final fulfillment'. Show which condition fails.
- Use one achievement per obligation without double fulfillment. Whether ONE achievement may fulfill MULTIPLE subject obligations is UNDECIDED Q51; DO NOT auto-apply across subjects. Schema should support future policy chosen.

11.3 SEPARATION / REHIRE:
- Permanent separation => freeze active-monitoring scope, record state as of separation (fulfilled/unresolved/already overdue), retain start/deadline, stop new post-separation overdue alerts Q41. Never erase old overdue findings.
- Rehire => new employment episode, new subject obligation on actual new teaching assignment; old obligation remains closed Q42. Lifetime certificates and course no-repeat restrictions persist.
- Q49: a relevant verified Silver certificate issued/verified AFTER separation may retrospectively establish historical fulfillment if qualifying exam was BEFORE effective separation, by original applicable deadline, eligibility/score/relevance verified. Must preserve the old status-as-of-separation snapshot and append new finding. Do not reopen monitoring or employment.
- Q50 also governs historical timeliness: timely actual exam, late certificate may be on-time recognition.
- Deadline 29-Feb calculation when anniversary year has no 29-Feb is NOT yet confirmed; implement safe policy-pending manual resolution/config flag rather than silently normalize to 28 Feb or 1 Mar. Document edge test.

11.4 SILVER MONITORING:
- Show per-faculty subject status, first assignment date, deadline, time-to-deadline, source assignment, linked evidence, relevance ruling, current/as-of historical assessment, resolution reason and employment episode.
- Faculty who stops teaching remains visible as outstanding until fulfilled/separated Q40.
- If course certificate remains pending after deadline, show result evidence still pending and no confirmed success, with alerts proportionate to institutional governance; the exact punitive policy for indefinitely pending provider results is unresolved.
- Reports must distinguish CURRENT ACTIVE faculty obligations from FORMER faculty historical closed obligations and show accurate denominators.

12.3 SEPARATION Q43–Q47:
  Policy lookup must use authoritative effective separation date versus source-precision commencement date, and whether external enrollment was actually verified. Effective date/known-at/reviewer decision time all different.

  Q44: enrolled + pre-commencement -> HoD closure/release; seat HELD until reviewer acts, even if reviewer acts after commencement (Q46).
  Q45: separation after commencement -> automatic close/release, specific exception to voluntary withdrawal retention.
  Q47: SAME DATE with unknown real order -> no invented midnight ordering; HoD chronology evidence review, hold capacity. If indeterminate after review -> explicit authorized closure/release under Q47, indeterminate finding. Do not falsely label Q44/45.
  If HR separation and course commencement have conflicting exact timestamp sources, rely on source precision/authority and route to reconciliation before automatic action.
  If no authorized HoD, preserve task+quota and alert under Q18. If applicant was HoD and no independent HoD exists, prohibit self-review and leave authority conflict pending, rather than invent permissions.
  Any after-the-fact correction of commencement or separation date may affect already-released seats; do NOT simply increment capacity again if a replacement application has consumed it. Use a controlled correction/reconciliation case and keep audit trail.

12.4 TIME MODEL:
  For every meaningful source fact track: business event effective date/time (possibly DATE-only); time zone and precision, upstream source and revision; AKUS recorded_at; evidence verified_at; decision committed_at; report-as-of cutoff. Do not convert DATE-only to fabricated 00:00 to order same-day events. Use UTC for precise instants and institutional local date semantics with Asia/Kolkata as deployment default; keep date-only values as dates. For NPTEL provider dates, preserve source timezone if provided.
  Boundaries 'on or before Silver deadline' Q50, 'before external enrollment' Q21, 'before commencement' Q43/44 and 'after commencement' Q45 need explicit comparison semantics; uncertain exact same-day ordering governed by Q47 where relevant, not guesses.
  Historical decisions must be reproducible given evidence and policy effective at that time. Avoid falsely calling all data 'bitemporal' if not actually storing both valid time and transaction time; implement those fields and queries correctly.
